Foods low in sodium

100 whole foods with the least sodium per serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Foods with none at all are listed first and sorted by overall nutrient density, so the most nutritious sodium-free foods lead.

Sodium is essential in small amounts but most diets contain far more than the 2,300 mg Daily Value, mainly from processed and restaurant food. Whole foods are naturally low in sodium.

Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for sodium is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which foods have no sodium?

Oranges, raw (California, valencias), Grapefruit, raw (pink and red, Florida), Beans, navy (mature seeds, cooked, boiled, without salt), Nuts, hazelnuts or filberts (blanched), Pasta, gluten-free (corn, cooked) and many other whole foods contain no measurable sodium. The list sorts sodium-free foods by overall nutrient density.

What is the daily limit for sodium?

The Daily Value is 2,300 mg; FDA guidance is to stay at or below it.
