How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). The pool is 1,677 whole foods: fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts and seeds, grains, fish, meat, poultry, dairy and eggs. Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for sugars is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which foods have no sugars?

Beef, variety meats and by-products (liver, cooked, pan-fried), Turkey, all classes (liver, cooked, simmered), Mollusks, oyster (eastern, canned), Mollusks, clam (mixed species, canned, drained solids), Crustaceans, crab (blue, cooked, moist heat) and many other whole foods contain no measurable sugars. The list sorts sugars-free foods by overall nutrient density.
