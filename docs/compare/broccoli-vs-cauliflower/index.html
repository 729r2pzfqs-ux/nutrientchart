Broccoli vs Cauliflower: nutrition compared

Broccoli has over 6× the vitamin K, over 2× the copper, 1.9× the riboflavin of Cauliflower per 100 g. Cauliflower has over 2× the choline, 1.5× the folate of Broccoli. Cauliflower is lower in calories (25 vs 34 kcal per 100 g).

Frequently asked questions

Which is healthier, Broccoli or Cauliflower?

Broccoli has over 6× the vitamin K, over 2× the copper, 1.9× the riboflavin of Cauliflower per 100 g. Cauliflower has over 2× the choline, 1.5× the folate of Broccoli. Cauliflower is lower in calories (25 vs 34 kcal per 100 g). By nutrient density (NRF9.3 per 100 kcal) Broccoli scores 186 and Cauliflower scores 172.
