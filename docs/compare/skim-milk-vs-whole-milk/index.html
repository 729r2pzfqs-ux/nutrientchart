Frequently asked questions

Which is healthier, Skim milk or Whole milk?

Skim milk has over 2× the vitamin A of Whole milk per 100 g. Skim milk is lower in calories (34 vs 61 kcal per 100 g). By nutrient density (NRF9.3 per 100 kcal) Skim milk scores 55 and Whole milk scores 8. Which is 'healthier' depends on what you need more of.

Which has more protein, Skim milk or Whole milk?

Skim milk: 3.4 g per 100 g for Skim milk vs 3.3 g for Whole milk (8.4 vs 8 g per serving).

Which has fewer calories, Skim milk or Whole milk?

Skim milk: 34 kcal per 100 g for Skim milk vs 61 kcal for Whole milk (83 vs 149 kcal per serving).
