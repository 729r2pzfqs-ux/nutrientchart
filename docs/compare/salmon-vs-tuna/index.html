Frequently asked questions

Which is healthier, Salmon or Tuna?

Salmon has over 11× the thiamin, over 10× the vitamin D, over 8× the folate of Tuna per 100 g. Tuna has over 4× the iron, 1.6× the selenium, 1.5× the zinc of Salmon. Tuna is lower in calories (86 vs 206 kcal per 100 g). By nutrient density (NRF9.3 per 100 kcal) Salmon scores 33 and Tuna scores 60. Which is 'healthier' depends on what you need more of.

Which has more protein, Salmon or Tuna?

Salmon: 22.1 g per 100 g for Salmon vs 19 g for Tuna (18.8 vs 16.1 g per serving).

Which has fewer calories, Salmon or Tuna?

Tuna: 206 kcal per 100 g for Salmon vs 86 kcal for Tuna (175 vs 73 kcal per serving).
