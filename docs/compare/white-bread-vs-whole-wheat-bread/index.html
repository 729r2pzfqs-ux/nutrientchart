White bread has over 2× the folate, over 2× the calcium, 1.9× the riboflavin of Whole wheat bread per 100 g. Whole wheat bread has over 12× the vitamin E, over 3× the manganese, over 3× the magnesium of White bread. Whole wheat bread is lower in calories (252 vs 266 kcal per 100 g).
