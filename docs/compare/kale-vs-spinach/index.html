Frequently asked questions

Which is healthier, Kale or Spinach?

Kale has over 3× the vitamin C, over 2× the manganese, 1.8× the riboflavin of Spinach per 100 g. Spinach has over 2× the magnesium, 1.8× the folate, 1.3× the vitamin A of Kale. Spinach is lower in calories (23 vs 35 kcal per 100 g). By nutrient density (NRF9.3 per 100 kcal) Kale scores 357 and Spinach scores 470. Which is 'healthier' depends on what you need more of.
