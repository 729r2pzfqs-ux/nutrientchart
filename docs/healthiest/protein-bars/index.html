How the score works. Score = NRF9.3 nutrient density per 100 kcal (sum of %DV for protein, fiber, vitamins A, C and E, calcium, iron, potassium and magnesium, each capped at 100%, minus %DV for saturated fat, added sugars (total sugars used as a proxy) and sodium), minus 10% of the %DV per 100 kcal for sugars and saturated fat as a category-specific tiebreaker. Per-100 kcal scoring favours foods that deliver nutrients for few calories; a food's absolute amounts per serving are in the table. Items are from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy (2018) and reflect the products USDA analysed; current retail formulations may differ. Full methodology.
