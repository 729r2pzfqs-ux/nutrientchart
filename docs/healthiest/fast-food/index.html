How the score works. Score = NRF9.3 nutrient density per 100 kcal (sum of %DV for protein, fiber, vitamins A, C and E, calcium, iron, potassium and magnesium, each capped at 100%, minus %DV for saturated fat, added sugars (total sugars used as a proxy) and sodium), minus 10% of the %DV per 100 kcal for sodium and saturated fat as a category-specific tiebreaker. Per-100 kcal scoring favours foods that deliver nutrients for few calories; a food's absolute amounts per serving are in the table. Items are from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy (2018) and reflect the products USDA analysed; current retail formulations may differ. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

What is the healthiest fast food?

By nutrient density per 100 calories (NRF9.3) Fast Foods, grilled chicken filet sandwich (with lettuce, tomato and spread) ranks first of 60 fast food items in the USDA database, with 419 calories per 1 sandwich. CHICK-FIL-A, Chick-n-Strips and WENDY'S, Ultimate Chicken Grill Sandwich follow. The score rewards protein, fiber, vitamins and minerals and penalises saturated fat, sugar and sodium.

Fast-food meals, sandwiches and entrées from the USDA database ranked by nutrient density per 100 calories (NRF9.3), with sodium and saturated fat as tiebreakers.
