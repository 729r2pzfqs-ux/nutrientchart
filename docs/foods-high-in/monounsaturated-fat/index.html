Foods high in monounsaturated fat (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by monounsaturated fat per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Pork leads with 25.2 g per 3 oz, followed by Avocados, raw (19.7 g) and Lamb, Australian (18 g).

Monounsaturated fats, the main fat in olive oil, avocados and most nuts, are associated with better blood lipid profiles when they replace saturated fat. There is no Daily Value; values here are grams per serving.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for monounsaturated fat is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in monounsaturated fat?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Pork, ground (72% lean / 28% fat, cooked, crumbles) has the most monounsaturated fat per serving: 25.2 g per 3 oz. Per 100 g it contains 29.6 g.

Which fruit has the most monounsaturated fat?

Avocados, raw (all commercial varieties), with 19.7 g per 1 avocado, NS as to Florida or California. See the full list of fruits high in monounsaturated fat below.

Which vegetable has the most monounsaturated fat?

Potato puffs, frozen (oven-heated), with 5.8 g per 1 cup. See the full list of vegetables high in monounsaturated fat below.

Is there a Daily Value for monounsaturated fat?

No. The FDA has not set a Daily Value for monounsaturated fat, so this list shows g per serving without a percentage. Monounsaturated fats, the main fat in olive oil, avocados and most nuts, are associated with better blood lipid profiles when they replace saturated fat.
