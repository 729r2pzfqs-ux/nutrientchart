Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in zinc?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Mollusks, oyster (eastern, canned) has the most zinc per serving: 77.3 mg per 3 oz (703% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 91 mg.

How much zinc do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 11 mg of zinc for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in zinc?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 5 of the 14 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
