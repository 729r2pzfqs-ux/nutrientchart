Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in zinc?

Among meat and poultry in the USDA database, Beef, chuck (short ribs, boneless, separable lean only, trimmed to 0" fat, select, cooked, braised) has the most zinc per serving: 10.4 mg per 3 oz (95% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 12.3 mg.

How much zinc do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 11 mg of zinc for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in zinc?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 40 of the 50 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
