Foods high in phosphorus (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by phosphorus per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Seeds, hemp seed leads with 468 mg per 1 oz (37% of the Daily Value), followed by Tempeh (442 mg) and Beef (422 mg).

Phosphorus is a component of bone, cell membranes and DNA. The Daily Value is 1,250 mg. Deficiency is rare; people with kidney disease may need to limit it.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for phosphorus is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in phosphorus?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Seeds, hemp seed (hulled) has the most phosphorus per serving: 468 mg per 1 oz (37% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 1,650 mg.

Which fruit has the most phosphorus?

Avocados, raw (Florida), with 122 mg per 1 fruit without skin and seeds (9.7% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in phosphorus below.

Which vegetable has the most phosphorus?

Edamame, frozen (prepared), with 304 mg per 1 cup (24% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in phosphorus below.

How much phosphorus do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 1,250 mg of phosphorus for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in phosphorus?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 42 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
