Foods high in manganese (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by manganese per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Teff, cooked leads with 7.21 mg per 1 cup (313% of the Daily Value), followed by Mollusks, mussel (5.78 mg) and Blueberries, wild (4.02 mg).

Manganese is a cofactor for enzymes involved in metabolism, bone formation and antioxidant defence. The Daily Value is 2.3 mg. Whole grains, nuts, legumes, leafy vegetables and tea are the main sources.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for manganese is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in manganese?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Teff, cooked has the most manganese per serving: 7.21 mg per 1 cup (313% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 2.86 mg.

Which fruit has the most manganese?

Blueberries, wild (frozen), with 4.02 mg per 1 cup, frozen (175% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in manganese below.

Which vegetable has the most manganese?

Garlic, raw, with 2.27 mg per 1 cup (99% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in manganese below.

How much manganese do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 2.3 mg of manganese for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in manganese?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 88 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
