Foods high in vitamin C (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by vitamin C per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Peppers, sweet leads with 258 mg per 1 pepper, large (3-3/4" long, 3" dia) (287% of the Daily Value), followed by Brussels sprouts, raw (126 mg) and Guavas, common (126 mg).

Vitamin C is an antioxidant needed to make collagen and to absorb plant iron. The Daily Value is 90 mg. Peppers, citrus, berries, kiwifruit and cruciferous vegetables are the richest sources; cooking in water reduces it.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). The pool is 1,677 whole foods: fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts and seeds, grains, fish, meat, poultry, dairy and eggs. Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for vitamin C is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin C?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Peppers, sweet (yellow, raw) has the most vitamin C per serving: 258 mg per 1 pepper, large (3-3/4" long, 3" dia) (287% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 139 mg.

Which fruit has the most vitamin C?

Guavas, common (raw), with 126 mg per 1 fruit, without refuse (140% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in vitamin C below.

Which vegetable has the most vitamin C?

Peppers, sweet (yellow, raw), with 258 mg per 1 pepper, large (3-3/4" long, 3" dia) (287% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in vitamin C below.

How much vitamin C do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 90 mg of vitamin C for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin C?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 79 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
