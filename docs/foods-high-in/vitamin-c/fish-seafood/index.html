Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin C?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Mollusks, clam (mixed species, cooked, moist heat) has the most vitamin C per serving: 18.8 mg per 3 oz (21% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 22.1 mg.

How much vitamin C do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 90 mg of vitamin C for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin C?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 1 of the 11 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
