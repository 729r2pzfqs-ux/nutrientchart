Foods high in vitamin B6 (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by vitamin B6 per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Garlic, raw leads with 1.68 mg per 1 cup (99% of the Daily Value), followed by Chickpeas (garbanzo beans, bengal gram) (1.14 mg) and Chicken, broiler or fryers (0.98 mg).

Vitamin B6 is involved in amino acid metabolism, red blood cell formation and immune function. The Daily Value is 1.7 mg. Fish, poultry, potatoes, bananas, chickpeas and fortified cereals are the main sources.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin B6?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Garlic, raw has the most vitamin B6 per serving: 1.68 mg per 1 cup (99% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 1.24 mg.

Which fruit has the most vitamin B6?

Avocados, raw (all commercial varieties), with 0.52 mg per 1 avocado, NS as to Florida or California (30% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in vitamin B6 below.

Which vegetable has the most vitamin B6?

Garlic, raw, with 1.68 mg per 1 cup (99% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in vitamin B6 below.

How much vitamin B6 do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 1.7 mg of vitamin B6 for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin B6?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 68 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
