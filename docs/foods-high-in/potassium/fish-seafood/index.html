Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in potassium?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Mollusks, clam (mixed species, canned, drained solids) has the most potassium per serving: 534 mg per 3 oz (11% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 628 mg.

How much potassium do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 4,700 mg of potassium for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in potassium?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 0 of the 14 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
