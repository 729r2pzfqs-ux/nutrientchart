Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in potassium?

Among legumes in the USDA database, Beans, adzuki (mature seeds, cooked, boiled, without salt) has the most potassium per serving: 1,224 mg per 1 cup (26% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 532 mg.

How much potassium do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 4,700 mg of potassium for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in potassium?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 3 of the 40 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
