Foods high in potassium (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by potassium per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Beet greens, cooked leads with 1,309 mg per 1 cup (1" pieces) (28% of the Daily Value), followed by Beans, adzuki (1,224 mg) and Beans, white (1,189 mg).

Potassium is an electrolyte that helps regulate fluid balance, nerve signals and muscle contractions, and a higher intake is linked to lower blood pressure. The Daily Value is 4,700 mg, which most people do not reach. Beans, potatoes, leafy greens, fish, dairy and fruit are the main sources.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). The pool is 1,677 whole foods: fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts and seeds, grains, fish, meat, poultry, dairy and eggs. Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for potassium is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in potassium?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Beet greens, cooked (boiled, drained, without salt) has the most potassium per serving: 1,309 mg per 1 cup (1" pieces) (28% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 909 mg.

Which fruit has the most potassium?

Avocados, raw (Florida), with 1,067 mg per 1 fruit without skin and seeds (23% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in potassium below.

Which vegetable has the most potassium?

Beet greens, cooked (boiled, drained, without salt), with 1,309 mg per 1 cup (1" pieces) (28% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in potassium below.

How much potassium do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 4,700 mg of potassium for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in potassium?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 9 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
