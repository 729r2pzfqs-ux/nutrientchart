Foods high in choline (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by choline per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Beef leads with 362 mg per 3 oz (66% of the Daily Value), followed by Vermicelli, made from soy (248 mg) and Turkey, all classes (187 mg).

Choline is needed for cell membranes, the neurotransmitter acetylcholine and fat transport from the liver. The Daily Value is 550 mg. Eggs, liver, meat, fish, soybeans and cruciferous vegetables are the main sources.

Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for choline is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in choline?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Beef, variety meats and by-products (liver, cooked, braised) has the most choline per serving: 362 mg per 3 oz (66% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 426 mg.

Which fruit has the most choline?

Avocados, raw (all commercial varieties), with 29 mg per 1 avocado, NS as to Florida or California (5.2% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in choline below.

Which vegetable has the most choline?

Corn pudding, home prepared, with 139 mg per 1 cup (25% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in choline below.

How much choline do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 550 mg of choline for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in choline?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 15 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
