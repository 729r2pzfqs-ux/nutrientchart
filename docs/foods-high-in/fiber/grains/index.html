Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in fiber?

Among grains in the USDA database, Bulgur, cooked has the most fiber per serving: 8.2 g per 1 cup (29% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 4.5 g.

How much fiber do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 28 g of fiber for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in fiber?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 7 of the 15 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
