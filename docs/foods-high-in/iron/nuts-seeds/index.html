Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in iron?

Among nuts and seeds in the USDA database, Seeds, sesame seeds (whole, roasted and toasted) has the most iron per serving: 4.2 mg per 1 oz (23% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 14.8 mg.

How much iron do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 18 mg of iron for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in iron?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 1 of the 12 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
