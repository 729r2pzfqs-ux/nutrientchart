Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in iron?

Among legumes in the USDA database, Soybeans, mature cooked (boiled, without salt) has the most iron per serving: 8.8 mg per 1 cup (49% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 5.1 mg.

How much iron do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 18 mg of iron for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in iron?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 13 of the 40 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
