How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in iron?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Mollusks, oyster (eastern, wild, cooked, moist heat) has the most iron per serving: 7.8 mg per 3 oz (44% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 9.2 mg.

How much iron do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 18 mg of iron for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in iron?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 2 of the 14 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
