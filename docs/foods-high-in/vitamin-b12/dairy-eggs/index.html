Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin B12?

Among dairy and eggs in the USDA database, Yogurt, plain (skim milk) has the most vitamin B12 per serving: 1.49 µg per 1 cup (8 fl oz) (62% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 0.61 µg.

How much vitamin B12 do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 2.4 µg of vitamin B12 for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin B12?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 12 of the 17 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
