Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin B12?

Among meat and poultry in the USDA database, Beef, variety meats and by-products (liver, cooked, pan-fried) has the most vitamin B12 per serving: 70.66 µg per 3 oz (2,944% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 83.13 µg.

How much vitamin B12 do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 2.4 µg of vitamin B12 for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin B12?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 36 of the 48 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
