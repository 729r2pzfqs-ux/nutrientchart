Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin B12?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Mollusks, clam (mixed species, cooked, moist heat) has the most vitamin B12 per serving: 84.06 µg per 3 oz (3,502% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 98.89 µg.

How much vitamin B12 do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 2.4 µg of vitamin B12 for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin B12?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 14 of the 14 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
