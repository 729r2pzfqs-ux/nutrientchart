Foods high in calcium (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by calcium per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Tofu, raw leads with 861 mg per 0.5 cup (66% of the Daily Value), followed by Yogurt (488 mg) and Cheese, parmesan (355 mg).

Calcium builds bone and teeth and is needed for muscle contraction and nerve signalling. The Daily Value is 1,300 mg. Dairy, canned fish with bones, tofu set with calcium and leafy greens are the main food sources.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). The pool is 1,677 whole foods: fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts and seeds, grains, fish, meat, poultry, dairy and eggs. Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for calcium is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in calcium?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Tofu, raw (firm, prepared with calcium sulfate) has the most calcium per serving: 861 mg per 0.5 cup (66% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 683 mg.

Which fruit has the most calcium?

Oranges, raw (Florida), with 61 mg per 1 fruit (2-5/8" dia) (4.7% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in calcium below.

Which vegetable has the most calcium?

Collards, cooked (boiled, drained, without salt), with 268 mg per 1 cup, chopped (21% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in calcium below.

How much calcium do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 1,300 mg of calcium for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in calcium?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 16 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
