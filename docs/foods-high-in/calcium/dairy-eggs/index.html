Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in calcium?

Among dairy and eggs in the USDA database, Yogurt, plain (skim milk) has the most calcium per serving: 488 mg per 1 cup (8 fl oz) (38% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 199 mg.

How much calcium do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 1,300 mg of calcium for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in calcium?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 6 of the 17 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
