Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in calcium?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Fish, sardine (Atlantic, canned in oil, drained solids with bone) has the most calcium per serving: 325 mg per 3 oz (25% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 382 mg.

How much calcium do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 1,300 mg of calcium for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in calcium?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 1 of the 14 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
