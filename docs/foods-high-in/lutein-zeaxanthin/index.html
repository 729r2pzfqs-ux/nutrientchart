Foods high in lutein + zeaxanthin (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by lutein + zeaxanthin per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Spinach, cooked leads with 20,354 µg per 1 cup, followed by Chard, swiss (19,276 µg) and Mustard greens, cooked (14,560 µg).

Lutein and zeaxanthin are the carotenoids concentrated in the retina and studied for eye health. There is no Daily Value; studies commonly use 6 to 10 mg a day. Kale, spinach, other dark greens, egg yolks and corn are the main sources.
