Foods high in folate (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by folate per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Turkey, all classes leads with 587 µg per 3 oz (147% of the Daily Value), followed by Arrowroot, raw (406 µg) and Beans, cranberry (roman) (366 µg).

Folate (vitamin B9) is needed for DNA synthesis and cell division, and is especially important before and during pregnancy. The Daily Value is 400 µg DFE. Legumes, leafy greens, asparagus, liver and fortified grains are the richest sources.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). The pool is 1,677 whole foods: fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts and seeds, grains, fish, meat, poultry, dairy and eggs. Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for folate is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in folate?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Turkey, all classes (liver, cooked, simmered) has the most folate per serving: 587 µg per 3 oz (147% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 691 µg.

Which fruit has the most folate?

Avocados, raw (all commercial varieties), with 163 µg per 1 avocado, NS as to Florida or California (41% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in folate below.

Which vegetable has the most folate?

Arrowroot, raw, with 406 µg per 1 cup, sliced (101% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in folate below.

How much folate do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 400 µg of folate for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in folate?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 44 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
