Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in protein?

Among legumes in the USDA database, Tempeh has the most protein per serving: 33.7 g per 1 cup (67% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 20.3 g.

How much protein do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 50 g of protein for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in protein?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 20 of the 40 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
