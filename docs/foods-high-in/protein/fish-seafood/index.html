Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in protein?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Fish, tuna (fresh, bluefin, cooked, dry heat) has the most protein per serving: 25.4 g per 3 oz (51% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 29.9 g.

How much protein do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 50 g of protein for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in protein?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 14 of the 14 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
