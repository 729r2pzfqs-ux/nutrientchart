Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in protein?

Among nuts and seeds in the USDA database, Seeds, hemp seed (hulled) has the most protein per serving: 8.9 g per 1 oz (18% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 31.6 g.

How much protein do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 50 g of protein for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in protein?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 0 of the 12 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
