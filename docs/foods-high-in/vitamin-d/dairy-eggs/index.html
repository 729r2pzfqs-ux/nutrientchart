Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin D?

Among dairy and eggs in the USDA database, Milk, buttermilk (fluid, whole) has the most vitamin D per serving: 3.19 µg per 1 cup (16% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 1.3 µg.

How much vitamin D do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 20 µg of vitamin D for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin D?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 0 of the 14 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
