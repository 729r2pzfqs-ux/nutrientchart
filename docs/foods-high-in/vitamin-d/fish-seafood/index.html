Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin D?

Among fish and seafood in the USDA database, Salmon, sockeye (canned, drained solids, without skin and bones) has the most vitamin D per serving: 18.27 µg per 3 oz (91% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 21.5 µg.

How much vitamin D do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 20 µg of vitamin D for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin D?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 7 of the 9 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
