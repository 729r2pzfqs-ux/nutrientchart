Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin D?

Among vegetables in the USDA database, Mushrooms, brown (italian, or crimini, exposed to ultraviolet light, raw) has the most vitamin D per serving: 27.75 µg per 1 cup whole (139% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 31.9 µg.

How much vitamin D do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 20 µg of vitamin D for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin D?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 3 of the 10 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
