Foods high in beta-carotene (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by beta-carotene per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Sweet potato, cooked leads with 30,976 µg per 1 cup, mashed, followed by Carrots, frozen (11,971 µg) and Spinach, cooked (11,318 µg).

Beta-carotene is the orange plant pigment the body converts to vitamin A, and an antioxidant in its own right. There is no Daily Value. Sweet potatoes, carrots, pumpkin, leafy greens and cantaloupe are the richest sources.
