Foods high in lycopene (per serving)

15 whole foods ranked by lycopene per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Watermelon, raw leads with 12,962 µg per 1 wedge (approx 1/16 of melon), followed by Tomatoes, red (10,424 µg) and Tomatoes, crushed (6,178 µg).

Lycopene is the red carotenoid in tomatoes, watermelon, pink grapefruit and guava. It is an antioxidant studied for heart and prostate health. There is no Daily Value; values are micrograms per serving. Cooked tomato products supply far more than raw.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in lycopene?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Watermelon, raw has the most lycopene per serving: 12,962 µg per 1 wedge (approx 1/16 of melon). Per 100 g it contains 4,532 µg.

Is there a Daily Value for lycopene?

No. The FDA has not set a Daily Value for lycopene, so this list shows µg per serving without a percentage. Lycopene is the red carotenoid in tomatoes, watermelon, pink grapefruit and guava.
