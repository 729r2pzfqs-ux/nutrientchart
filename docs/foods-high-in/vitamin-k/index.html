Foods high in vitamin K (per serving)

100 whole foods ranked by vitamin K per common serving, from USDA FoodData Central. Parsley leads with 984 µg per 1 cup chopped (820% of the Daily Value), followed by Spinach, cooked (888 µg) and Mustard greens, cooked (830 µg).

Vitamin K is needed for blood clotting and bone metabolism. The Daily Value is 120 µg. Leafy greens (kale, spinach, collards), broccoli and Brussels sprouts contain very large amounts. People on warfarin should keep intake consistent.

How this list is built. Values are from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, with Foundation Foods analytical updates where available). The pool is 1,677 whole foods: fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts and seeds, grains, fish, meat, poultry, dairy and eggs. Spices, herbs, baby foods, powders, flours, dried and freeze-dried items, juices, processed meats, organ meats other than liver and uncommon foods are excluded; meats, fish, grains and legumes are ranked cooked. Each food family appears once and no single animal or plant fills more than six places. Ranking is by amount per serving; the per-100 g column is shown for reference. A food that USDA has not analysed for vitamin K is left out rather than counted as zero. Full methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Which food is highest in vitamin K?

Among whole foods in the USDA database, Parsley, fresh has the most vitamin K per serving: 984 µg per 1 cup chopped (820% of the Daily Value). Per 100 g it contains 1,640 µg.

Which fruit has the most vitamin K?

Pomegranates, raw, with 46.2 µg per 1 pomegranate (4" dia) (38% of the Daily Value). See the full list of fruits high in vitamin K below.

Which vegetable has the most vitamin K?

Parsley, fresh, with 984 µg per 1 cup chopped (820% of the Daily Value). See the full list of vegetables high in vitamin K below.

How much vitamin K do I need per day?

The FDA Daily Value used on Nutrition Facts labels is 120 µg of vitamin K for adults and children aged 4 and over. Individual needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy and health conditions.

What counts as a food high in vitamin K?

FDA labeling rules call a food a 'good source' when one serving provides 10 to 19% of the Daily Value and 'high' or 'excellent' at 20% or more. 53 of the 100 foods on this list reach 20% DV per serving.
