Zucchini nutrition facts

Zucchini (1 cup, chopped, 124 g) has 21 calories, 1.5 g protein, 1.2 g fiber, 0.4 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (25% DV) and a good source of vitamin B6.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in zucchini?

Zucchini has 21 calories per 1 cup, chopped (124 g), or 17 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in zucchini?

1.5 g of protein per 1 cup, chopped, which is 3% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is zucchini rich in?

Per serving, zucchini is richest in Vitamin C at 25% of the Daily Value, Vitamin B6 (12%), Manganese (9.5%), Riboflavin (B2) (9%).

Is zucchini healthy?

By nutrient density, zucchini scores 190 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
