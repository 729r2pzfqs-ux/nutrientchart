Black beans nutrition facts

Black beans (1 cup, 172 g) has 227 calories, 15.2 g protein, 15 g fiber, 0.9 g fat. It is an excellent source of folate (64% DV), fiber (53% DV), copper (40% DV) and a good source of phosphorus and zinc.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in black beans?

Black beans has 227 calories per 1 cup (172 g), or 132 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in black beans?

15.2 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 30% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is black beans rich in?

Per serving, black beans is richest in Folate (DFE) at 64% of the Daily Value, Fiber (53%), Copper (40%), Thiamin (B1) (35%).

Is black beans healthy?

By nutrient density, black beans scores 69 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
