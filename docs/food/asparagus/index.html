Asparagus nutrition facts

Asparagus (0.5 cup, 90 g) has 20 calories, 2.2 g protein, 1.8 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (38% DV), folate (34% DV) and a good source of copper and thiamin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in asparagus?

Asparagus has 20 calories per 0.5 cup (90 g), or 22 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in asparagus?

2.2 g of protein per 0.5 cup, which is 4.3% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is asparagus rich in?

Per serving, asparagus is richest in Vitamin K at 38% of the Daily Value, Folate (DFE) (34%), Copper (16%), Thiamin (B1) (12%).

Is asparagus healthy?

By nutrient density, asparagus scores 216 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
