Green beans nutrition facts

Green beans (1 cup, 125 g) has 44 calories, 2.4 g protein, 4 g fiber, 0.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (50% DV) and a good source of manganese and fiber.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in green beans?

Green beans has 44 calories per 1 cup (125 g), or 35 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in green beans?

2.4 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 4.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is green beans rich in?

Per serving, green beans is richest in Vitamin K at 50% of the Daily Value, Manganese (15%), Fiber (14%), Vitamin C (13%).

Is green beans healthy?

By nutrient density, green beans scores 112 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
