Pinto beans nutrition facts

Pinto beans (1 cup, 171 g) has 245 calories, 15.4 g protein, 15.4 g fiber, 1.1 g fat. It is an excellent source of folate (74% DV), fiber (55% DV), copper (42% DV) and a good source of iron and selenium.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in pinto beans?

Pinto beans has 245 calories per 1 cup (171 g), or 143 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in pinto beans?

15.4 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 31% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is pinto beans rich in?

Per serving, pinto beans is richest in Folate (DFE) at 74% of the Daily Value, Fiber (55%), Copper (42%), Manganese (34%).

Is pinto beans healthy?

By nutrient density, pinto beans scores 64 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
