Pork chop nutrition facts

Pork chop (3 oz, 85 g) has 153 calories, 22.7 g protein, 0 g fiber, 6.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of selenium (71% DV), protein (45% DV), niacin (45% DV) and a good source of vitamin B12 and zinc.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in pork chop?

Pork chop has 153 calories per 3 oz (85 g), or 180 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in pork chop?

22.7 g of protein per 3 oz, which is 45% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is pork chop rich in?

Per serving, pork chop is richest in Selenium at 71% of the Daily Value, Protein (45%), Niacin (B3) (45%), Thiamin (B1) (44%).

Is pork chop healthy?

By nutrient density, pork chop scores 34 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is average for whole foods compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
