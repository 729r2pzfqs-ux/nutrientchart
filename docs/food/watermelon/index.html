Watermelon nutrition facts

Watermelon (1 cup, balls, 154 g) has 46 calories, 0.9 g protein, 0.6 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is a good source of vitamin C.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in watermelon?

Watermelon has 46 calories per 1 cup, balls (154 g), or 30 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in watermelon?

0.9 g of protein per 1 cup, balls, which is 1.9% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is watermelon rich in?

Per serving, watermelon is richest in Vitamin C at 14% of the Daily Value, Copper (7.2%), Pantothenic acid (B5) (6.8%), Vitamin A (RAE) (4.8%).

Is watermelon healthy?

By nutrient density, watermelon scores 31 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
