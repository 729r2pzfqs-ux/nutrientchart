Cabbage nutrition facts

Cabbage (1 cup, chopped, 89 g) has 22 calories, 0.9 g protein, 2.2 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (44% DV), vitamin C (40% DV).

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in cabbage?

Cabbage has 22 calories per 1 cup, chopped (89 g), or 25 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in cabbage?

0.9 g of protein per 1 cup, chopped, which is 1.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is cabbage rich in?

Per serving, cabbage is richest in Vitamin K at 44% of the Daily Value, Vitamin C (40%), Manganese (9.6%), Folate (DFE) (9.6%).

Is cabbage healthy?

By nutrient density, cabbage scores 166 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
