Edamame nutrition facts

Edamame (1 cup, 155 g) has 188 calories, 18.5 g protein, 9.7 g fiber, 8.1 g fat. It is an excellent source of manganese (79% DV), folate (72% DV), copper (70% DV) and a good source of zinc and vitamin C.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in edamame?

Edamame has 188 calories per 1 cup (155 g), or 121 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in edamame?

18.5 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 37% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is edamame rich in?

Per serving, edamame is richest in Manganese at 79% of the Daily Value, Folate (DFE) (72%), Copper (70%), Protein (37%).

Is edamame healthy?

By nutrient density, edamame scores 84 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
