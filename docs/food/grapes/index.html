Grapes nutrition facts

Grapes (1 cup, 151 g) has 104 calories, 1.1 g protein, 1.4 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of copper (21% DV) and a good source of vitamin K.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in grapes?

Grapes has 104 calories per 1 cup (151 g), or 69 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in grapes?

1.1 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 2.2% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is grapes rich in?

Per serving, grapes is richest in Copper at 21% of the Daily Value, Vitamin K (18%), Thiamin (B1) (8.7%), Riboflavin (B2) (8.1%).

Is grapes healthy?

By nutrient density, grapes scores -19 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is average for whole foods compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
