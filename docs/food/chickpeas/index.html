Chickpeas nutrition facts

Chickpeas (1 cup, 164 g) has 269 calories, 14.5 g protein, 12.5 g fiber, 4.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of manganese (73% DV), folate (71% DV), copper (64% DV) and a good source of magnesium and thiamin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in chickpeas?

Chickpeas has 269 calories per 1 cup (164 g), or 164 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in chickpeas?

14.5 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 29% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is chickpeas rich in?

Per serving, chickpeas is richest in Manganese at 73% of the Daily Value, Folate (DFE) (71%), Copper (64%), Fiber (45%).

Is chickpeas healthy?

By nutrient density, chickpeas scores 46 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
