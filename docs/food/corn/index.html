Sweet corn nutrition facts

Sweet corn (1 ear medium (6-3/4" to 7-1/2" long), 103 g) has 99 calories, 3.5 g protein, 2.5 g fiber, 1.5 g fat. It is a good source of pantothenic acid and niacin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in sweet corn?

Sweet corn has 99 calories per 1 ear medium (6-3/4" to 7-1/2" long) (103 g), or 96 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in sweet corn?

3.5 g of protein per 1 ear medium (6-3/4" to 7-1/2" long), which is 7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is sweet corn rich in?

Per serving, sweet corn is richest in Pantothenic acid (B5) at 16% of the Daily Value, Niacin (B3) (11%), Fiber (8.8%), Vitamin B6 (8.4%).

Is sweet corn healthy?

By nutrient density, sweet corn scores 28 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
