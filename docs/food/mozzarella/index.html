Mozzarella nutrition facts

Mozzarella (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 72 calories, 6.9 g protein, 0 g fiber, 4.5 g fat. It is a good source of calcium and protein.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in mozzarella?

Mozzarella has 72 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 254 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in mozzarella?

6.9 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 14% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is mozzarella rich in?

Per serving, mozzarella is richest in Calcium at 17% of the Daily Value, Protein (14%), Phosphorus (11%), Vitamin B12 (9.7%).

Is mozzarella healthy?

By nutrient density, mozzarella scores 21 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is pro-inflammatory profile compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
