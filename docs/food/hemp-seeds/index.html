Hemp seeds nutrition facts

Hemp seeds (3 tbsp, 30 g) has 166 calories, 9.5 g protein, 1.2 g fiber, 14.6 g fat. It is an excellent source of manganese (99% DV), copper (53% DV), magnesium (50% DV) and a good source of protein and niacin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in hemp seeds?

Hemp seeds has 166 calories per 3 tbsp (30 g), or 553 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in hemp seeds?

9.5 g of protein per 3 tbsp, which is 19% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is hemp seeds rich in?

Per serving, hemp seeds is richest in Manganese at 99% of the Daily Value, Copper (53%), Magnesium (50%), Phosphorus (40%).

Is hemp seeds healthy?

By nutrient density, hemp seeds scores 54 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
