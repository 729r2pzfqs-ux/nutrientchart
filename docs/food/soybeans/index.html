Soybeans nutrition facts

Soybeans (1 cup, 172 g) has 296 calories, 31.3 g protein, 10.3 g fiber, 15.4 g fat. It is an excellent source of copper (78% DV), protein (63% DV), manganese (62% DV) and a good source of potassium and zinc.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in soybeans?

Soybeans has 296 calories per 1 cup (172 g), or 172 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in soybeans?

31.3 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 63% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is soybeans rich in?

Per serving, soybeans is richest in Copper at 78% of the Daily Value, Protein (63%), Manganese (62%), Iron (49%).

Is soybeans healthy?

By nutrient density, soybeans scores 68 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
