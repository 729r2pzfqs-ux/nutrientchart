Kidney beans nutrition facts

Kidney beans (1 cup, 177 g) has 225 calories, 15.3 g protein, 11.3 g fiber, 0.9 g fat. It is an excellent source of folate (58% DV), copper (42% DV), fiber (40% DV) and a good source of phosphorus and magnesium.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in kidney beans?

Kidney beans has 225 calories per 1 cup (177 g), or 127 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in kidney beans?

15.3 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 31% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is kidney beans rich in?

Per serving, kidney beans is richest in Folate (DFE) at 58% of the Daily Value, Copper (42%), Fiber (40%), Manganese (33%).

Is kidney beans healthy?

By nutrient density, kidney beans scores 58 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
