Yogurt (plain, whole milk) nutrition facts

Yogurt (1 container (6 oz), 170 g) has 104 calories, 6.5 g protein, 0 g fiber, 7.6 g fat. It is an excellent source of riboflavin (32% DV), vitamin B12 (26% DV) and a good source of calcium and phosphorus.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in yogurt?

Yogurt has 104 calories per 1 container (6 oz) (170 g), or 61 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in yogurt?

6.5 g of protein per 1 container (6 oz), which is 13% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is yogurt rich in?

Per serving, yogurt is richest in Riboflavin (B2) at 32% of the Daily Value, Vitamin B12 (26%), Calcium (17%), Phosphorus (14%).

Is yogurt healthy?

By nutrient density, yogurt scores 8 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is below average compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
