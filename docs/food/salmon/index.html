Salmon (Atlantic, farmed) nutrition facts

Salmon (3 oz, 85 g) has 175 calories, 18.8 g protein, 0 g fiber, 10.5 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin B12 (99% DV), selenium (64% DV), vitamin D (56% DV) and a good source of phosphorus and choline.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in salmon?

Salmon has 175 calories per 3 oz (85 g), or 206 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in salmon?

18.8 g of protein per 3 oz, which is 38% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is salmon rich in?

Per serving, salmon is richest in Vitamin B12 at 99% of the Daily Value, Selenium (64%), Vitamin D (56%), Niacin (B3) (43%).

Is salmon healthy?

By nutrient density, salmon scores 33 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
