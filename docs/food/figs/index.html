Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in figs?

Figs has 37 calories per 1 medium (2-1/4" dia) (50 g), or 74 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in figs?

0.4 g of protein per 1 medium (2-1/4" dia), which is 0.8% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is figs rich in?

Per serving, figs is richest in Fiber at 5.2% of the Daily Value, Copper (3.9%), Vitamin B6 (3.3%), Pantothenic acid (B5) (3%).

Is figs healthy?

By nutrient density, figs scores -5 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
