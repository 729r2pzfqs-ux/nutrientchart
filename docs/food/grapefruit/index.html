Grapefruit nutrition facts

Grapefruit (0.5 fruit (3-3/4" dia), 123 g) has 52 calories, 0.9 g protein, 2 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (43% DV).

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in grapefruit?

Grapefruit has 52 calories per 0.5 fruit (3-3/4" dia) (123 g), or 42 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in grapefruit?

0.9 g of protein per 0.5 fruit (3-3/4" dia), which is 1.9% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is grapefruit rich in?

Per serving, grapefruit is richest in Vitamin C at 43% of the Daily Value, Vitamin A (RAE) (7.9%), Fiber (7%), Pantothenic acid (B5) (6.4%).

Is grapefruit healthy?

By nutrient density, grapefruit scores 101 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
