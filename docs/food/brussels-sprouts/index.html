Brussels sprouts nutrition facts

Brussels sprouts (0.5 cup, 78 g) has 28 calories, 2 g protein, 2 g fiber, 0.4 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (91% DV), vitamin C (54% DV) and a good source of folate.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in Brussels sprouts?

Brussels sprouts has 28 calories per 0.5 cup (78 g), or 36 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in Brussels sprouts?

2 g of protein per 0.5 cup, which is 4% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is Brussels sprouts rich in?

Per serving, Brussels sprouts is richest in Vitamin K at 91% of the Daily Value, Vitamin C (54%), Folate (DFE) (12%), Vitamin B6 (8.2%).

Is Brussels sprouts healthy?

By nutrient density, Brussels sprouts scores 204 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
