Raisins nutrition facts

Raisins (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 85 calories, 0.9 g protein, 1 g fiber, 0.1 g fat. It is a good source of copper.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in raisins?

Raisins has 85 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 299 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in raisins?

0.9 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 1.9% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is raisins rich in?

Per serving, raisins is richest in Copper at 10% of the Daily Value, Potassium (5.6%), Vitamin B6 (3.9%), Fiber (3.7%).

Is raisins healthy?

By nutrient density, raisins scores -22 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is pro-inflammatory profile compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
