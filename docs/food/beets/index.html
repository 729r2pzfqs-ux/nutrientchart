Beets nutrition facts

Beets (1 cup, 136 g) has 58 calories, 2.3 g protein, 4.2 g fiber, 0.4 g fat. It is an excellent source of folate (35% DV), copper (24% DV) and a good source of manganese and fiber.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in beets?

Beets has 58 calories per 1 cup (136 g), or 43 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in beets?

2.3 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 4.6% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is beets rich in?

Per serving, beets is richest in Folate (DFE) at 35% of the Daily Value, Copper (24%), Manganese (15%), Fiber (15%).

Is beets healthy?

By nutrient density, beets scores 40 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
