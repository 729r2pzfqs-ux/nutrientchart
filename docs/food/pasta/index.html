Pasta nutrition facts

Pasta (1 cup spaghetti not packed, 124 g) has 196 calories, 7.2 g protein, 2.2 g fiber, 1.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of selenium (60% DV), folate (37% DV), thiamin (28% DV) and a good source of manganese and protein.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in pasta?

Pasta has 196 calories per 1 cup spaghetti not packed (124 g), or 158 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in pasta?

7.2 g of protein per 1 cup spaghetti not packed, which is 14% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is pasta rich in?

Per serving, pasta is richest in Selenium at 60% of the Daily Value, Folate (DFE) (37%), Thiamin (B1) (28%), Manganese (17%).

Is pasta healthy?

By nutrient density, pasta scores 18 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is average for whole foods compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
