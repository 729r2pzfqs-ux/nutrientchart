Chia seeds nutrition facts

Chia seeds (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 138 calories, 4.8 g protein, 9.8 g fiber, 9.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of manganese (93% DV), copper (49% DV), fiber (35% DV) and a good source of phosphorus and niacin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in chia seeds?

Chia seeds has 138 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 486 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in chia seeds?

4.8 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 9.6% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is chia seeds rich in?

Per serving, chia seeds is richest in Manganese at 93% of the Daily Value, Copper (49%), Fiber (35%), Selenium (28%).

Is chia seeds healthy?

By nutrient density, chia seeds scores 65 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
