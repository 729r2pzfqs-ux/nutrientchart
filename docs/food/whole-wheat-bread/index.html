Whole wheat bread nutrition facts

Whole wheat bread (1 slice, 32 g) has 81 calories, 4 g protein, 1.9 g fiber, 1.1 g fat. It is an excellent source of manganese (26% DV) and a good source of selenium and niacin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in whole wheat bread?

Whole wheat bread has 81 calories per 1 slice (32 g), or 252 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in whole wheat bread?

4 g of protein per 1 slice, which is 8% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is whole wheat bread rich in?

Per serving, whole wheat bread is richest in Manganese at 26% of the Daily Value, Selenium (15%), Niacin (B3) (10%), Copper (9%).

Is whole wheat bread healthy?

By nutrient density, whole wheat bread scores 32 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
