Cottage cheese (2%) nutrition facts

Cottage cheese (1 cup (not packed), 226 g) has 183 calories, 24.9 g protein, 0 g fiber, 5.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of selenium (60% DV), protein (50% DV), riboflavin (41% DV) and a good source of calcium and vitamin A.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in cottage cheese?

Cottage cheese has 183 calories per 1 cup (not packed) (226 g), or 81 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in cottage cheese?

24.9 g of protein per 1 cup (not packed), which is 50% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is cottage cheese rich in?

Per serving, cottage cheese is richest in Selenium at 60% of the Daily Value, Protein (50%), Riboflavin (B2) (41%), Vitamin B12 (40%).

Is cottage cheese healthy?

By nutrient density, cottage cheese scores 19 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is pro-inflammatory profile compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
