Hazelnuts nutrition facts

Hazelnuts (1 oz (21 whole kernels), 28.35 g) has 178 calories, 3.8 g protein, 2.4 g fiber, 15.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of manganese (75% DV), copper (45% DV), vitamin E (28% DV) and a good source of thiamin and magnesium.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in hazelnuts?

Hazelnuts has 178 calories per 1 oz (21 whole kernels) (28.35 g), or 628 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in hazelnuts?

3.8 g of protein per 1 oz (21 whole kernels), which is 7.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is hazelnuts rich in?

Per serving, hazelnuts is richest in Manganese at 75% of the Daily Value, Copper (45%), Vitamin E (28%), Thiamin (B1) (15%).

Is hazelnuts healthy?

By nutrient density, hazelnuts scores 34 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
