Butternut squash nutrition facts

Butternut squash (1 cup, cubes, 205 g) has 82 calories, 1.8 g protein, 6.6 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin A (127% DV), vitamin C (34% DV), fiber (23% DV) and a good source of vitamin E and manganese.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in butternut squash?

Butternut squash has 82 calories per 1 cup, cubes (205 g), or 40 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in butternut squash?

1.8 g of protein per 1 cup, cubes, which is 3.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is butternut squash rich in?

Per serving, butternut squash is richest in Vitamin A (RAE) at 127% of the Daily Value, Vitamin C (34%), Fiber (23%), Vitamin E (18%).

Is butternut squash healthy?

By nutrient density, butternut squash scores 235 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
