Mango nutrition facts

Mango (1 cup pieces, 165 g) has 99 calories, 1.4 g protein, 2.6 g fiber, 0.6 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (67% DV), copper (20% DV) and a good source of folate and vitamin B6.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in mango?

Mango has 99 calories per 1 cup pieces (165 g), or 60 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in mango?

1.4 g of protein per 1 cup pieces, which is 2.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is mango rich in?

Per serving, mango is richest in Vitamin C at 67% of the Daily Value, Copper (20%), Folate (DFE) (18%), Vitamin B6 (12%).

Is mango healthy?

By nutrient density, mango scores 66 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
