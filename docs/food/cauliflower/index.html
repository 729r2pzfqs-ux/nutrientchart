Cauliflower nutrition facts

Cauliflower (1 cup chopped (1/2" pieces), 107 g) has 27 calories, 1.8 g protein, 2.1 g fiber, 0.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (80% DV), folate (26% DV) and a good source of pantothenic acid and vitamin K.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in cauliflower?

Cauliflower has 27 calories per 1 cup chopped (1/2" pieces) (107 g), or 25 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in cauliflower?

1.8 g of protein per 1 cup chopped (1/2" pieces), which is 3.5% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is cauliflower rich in?

Per serving, cauliflower is richest in Vitamin C at 80% of the Daily Value, Folate (DFE) (26%), Pantothenic acid (B5) (14%), Vitamin K (14%).

Is cauliflower healthy?

By nutrient density, cauliflower scores 172 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
