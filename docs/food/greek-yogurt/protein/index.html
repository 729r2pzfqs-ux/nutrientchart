Protein in Greek yogurt

Greek yogurt has 17.5 g of protein per 1 container (170 g), which is 35% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 10.3 g. Among dairy and eggs it ranks #1 of 17 for protein per serving, and #68 of 100 across all whole foods.

About protein

Protein supplies the amino acids used to build and repair muscle, skin, enzymes and hormones. The Daily Value is 50 g; needs rise with body weight, age and training.

Frequently asked questions

How much protein is in Greek yogurt?

Greek yogurt has 17.5 g of protein per 1 container (170 g), which is 35% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 10.3 g.

Is Greek yogurt high in protein?

Yes. At 35% of the Daily Value per serving, Greek yogurt counts as an excellent source under FDA labeling rules (10% is 'good', 20% is 'high'). Among dairy and eggs it ranks #1 of 17 for protein per serving, and #68 of 100 across all whole foods.

How much protein do I need a day?

The FDA Daily Value is 50 g. One 1 container of Greek yogurt covers 35% of it.
