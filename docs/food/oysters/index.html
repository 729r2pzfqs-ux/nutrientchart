Oysters nutrition facts

Oysters (3 oz, 85 g) has 87 calories, 9.7 g protein, 0 g fiber, 2.9 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin B12 (620% DV), zinc (607% DV), copper (539% DV) and a good source of protein and phosphorus.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in oysters?

Oysters has 87 calories per 3 oz (85 g), or 102 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in oysters?

9.7 g of protein per 3 oz, which is 19% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is oysters rich in?

Per serving, oysters is richest in Vitamin B12 at 620% of the Daily Value, Zinc (607%), Copper (539%), Selenium (61%).

Is oysters healthy?

By nutrient density, oysters scores 92 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
