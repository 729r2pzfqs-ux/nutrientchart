About zinc

Zinc supports immune function, wound healing, taste and smell, and protein and DNA synthesis. The Daily Value is 11 mg. Oysters, red meat, poultry, beans, nuts and whole grains are good sources; zinc from plants is less well absorbed.

Frequently asked questions

How much zinc is in oysters?

Oysters has 66.8 mg of zinc per 3 oz (85 g), which is 607% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 78.6 mg.

Is oysters high in zinc?

Yes. At 607% of the Daily Value per serving, oysters counts as an excellent source under FDA labeling rules (10% is 'good', 20% is 'high').

How much zinc do I need a day?

The FDA Daily Value is 11 mg. One 3 oz of oysters covers 607% of it.
