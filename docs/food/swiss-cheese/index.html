Swiss cheese nutrition facts

Swiss cheese (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 111 calories, 7.7 g protein, 0 g fiber, 8.8 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin B12 (36% DV) and a good source of calcium and selenium.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in Swiss cheese?

Swiss cheese has 111 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 393 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in Swiss cheese?

7.7 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 15% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is Swiss cheese rich in?

Per serving, Swiss cheese is richest in Vitamin B12 at 36% of the Daily Value, Calcium (19%), Selenium (16%), Protein (15%).

Is Swiss cheese healthy?

By nutrient density, Swiss cheese scores 18 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is pro-inflammatory profile compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
