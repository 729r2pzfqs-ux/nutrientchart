Orange nutrition facts

Orange (1 fruit (2-5/8" dia), 131 g) has 62 calories, 1.2 g protein, 3.1 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (77% DV) and a good source of fiber.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in orange?

Orange has 62 calories per 1 fruit (2-5/8" dia) (131 g), or 47 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in orange?

1.2 g of protein per 1 fruit (2-5/8" dia), which is 2.5% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is orange rich in?

Per serving, orange is richest in Vitamin C at 77% of the Daily Value, Fiber (11%), Folate (DFE) (9.8%), Thiamin (B1) (9.5%).

Is orange healthy?

By nutrient density, orange scores 108 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
