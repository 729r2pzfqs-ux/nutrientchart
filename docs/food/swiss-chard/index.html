Swiss chard nutrition facts

Swiss chard (1 cup, chopped, 175 g) has 35 calories, 3.3 g protein, 3.7 g fiber, 0.1 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (477% DV), vitamin A (60% DV), magnesium (36% DV) and a good source of fiber and riboflavin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in Swiss chard?

Swiss chard has 35 calories per 1 cup, chopped (175 g), or 20 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in Swiss chard?

3.3 g of protein per 1 cup, chopped, which is 6.6% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is Swiss chard rich in?

Per serving, Swiss chard is richest in Vitamin K at 477% of the Daily Value, Vitamin A (RAE) (60%), Magnesium (36%), Vitamin C (35%).

Is Swiss chard healthy?

By nutrient density, Swiss chard scores 513 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
