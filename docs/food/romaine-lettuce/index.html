Romaine lettuce nutrition facts

Romaine lettuce (1 cup shredded, 47 g) has 8 calories, 0.6 g protein, 0.8 g fiber, 0.1 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (40% DV), vitamin A (23% DV).

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in romaine lettuce?

Romaine lettuce has 8 calories per 1 cup shredded (47 g), or 17 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in romaine lettuce?

0.6 g of protein per 1 cup shredded, which is 1.2% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is romaine lettuce rich in?

Per serving, romaine lettuce is richest in Vitamin K at 40% of the Daily Value, Vitamin A (RAE) (23%), Folate (DFE) (5.9%), Thiamin (B1) (3.1%).

Is romaine lettuce healthy?

By nutrient density, romaine lettuce scores 268 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
