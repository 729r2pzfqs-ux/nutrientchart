Fiber in avocado

Avocado has 10.1 g of fiber per 1 cup, cubes (150 g), which is 36% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 6.7 g.

About fiber

Dietary fiber is the part of plant foods that is not digested. It supports regular digestion, feeds gut bacteria and helps moderate blood sugar and cholesterol. The Daily Value is 28 g; most people get about half that.

Frequently asked questions

How much fiber is in avocado?

Avocado has 10.1 g of fiber per 1 cup, cubes (150 g), which is 36% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 6.7 g.

Is avocado high in fiber?

Yes. At 36% of the Daily Value per serving, avocado counts as an excellent source under FDA labeling rules (10% is 'good', 20% is 'high').

How much fiber do I need a day?

The FDA Daily Value is 28 g. One 1 cup, cubes of avocado covers 36% of it.
