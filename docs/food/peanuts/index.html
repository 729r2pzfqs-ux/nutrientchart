Peanuts nutrition facts

Peanuts (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 161 calories, 6.6 g protein, 2.3 g fiber, 12.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of niacin (21% DV), manganese (21% DV) and a good source of folate and vitamin E.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in peanuts?

Peanuts has 161 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 567 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in peanuts?

6.6 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 13% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is peanuts rich in?

Per serving, peanuts is richest in Niacin (B3) at 21% of the Daily Value, Manganese (21%), Folate (DFE) (17%), Vitamin E (16%).

Is peanuts healthy?

By nutrient density, peanuts scores 28 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
