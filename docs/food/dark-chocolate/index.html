Dark chocolate (70–85%) nutrition facts

Dark chocolate (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 170 calories, 2.2 g protein, 3.1 g fiber, 12.1 g fat. It is an excellent source of copper (56% DV), manganese (24% DV) and a good source of iron and magnesium.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in dark chocolate?

Dark chocolate has 170 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 598 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in dark chocolate?

2.2 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 4.4% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is dark chocolate rich in?

Per serving, dark chocolate is richest in Copper at 56% of the Daily Value, Manganese (24%), Iron (19%), Magnesium (15%).

Is dark chocolate healthy?

By nutrient density, dark chocolate scores 5 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is average for whole foods compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
