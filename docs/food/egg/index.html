Egg nutrition facts

Egg (1 large, 50 g) has 78 calories, 6.3 g protein, 0 g fiber, 5.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of selenium (28% DV), choline (27% DV), vitamin B12 (23% DV) and a good source of riboflavin and pantothenic acid.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in egg?

Egg has 78 calories per 1 large (50 g), or 155 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in egg?

6.3 g of protein per 1 large, which is 13% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is egg rich in?

Per serving, egg is richest in Selenium at 28% of the Daily Value, Choline (27%), Vitamin B12 (23%), Riboflavin (B2) (20%).

Is egg healthy?

By nutrient density, egg scores 26 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is average for whole foods compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
