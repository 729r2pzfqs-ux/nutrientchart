Tofu (firm) nutrition facts

Tofu (0.5 cup, 126 g) has 181 calories, 21.8 g protein, 2.9 g fiber, 11 g fat. It is an excellent source of calcium (66% DV), manganese (65% DV), copper (53% DV) and a good source of phosphorus and iron.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in tofu?

Tofu has 181 calories per 0.5 cup (126 g), or 144 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in tofu?

21.8 g of protein per 0.5 cup, which is 44% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is tofu rich in?

Per serving, tofu is richest in Calcium at 66% of the Daily Value, Manganese (65%), Copper (53%), Protein (44%).

Is tofu healthy?

By nutrient density, tofu scores 85 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
