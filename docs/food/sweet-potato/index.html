Sweet potato nutrition facts

Sweet potato (1 medium (2" dia, 5" long, raw), 114 g) has 103 calories, 2.3 g protein, 3.8 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin A (122% DV), vitamin C (25% DV), manganese (25% DV) and a good source of vitamin B6 and fiber.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in sweet potato?

Sweet potato has 103 calories per 1 medium (2" dia, 5" long, raw) (114 g), or 90 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in sweet potato?

2.3 g of protein per 1 medium (2" dia, 5" long, raw), which is 4.6% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is sweet potato rich in?

Per serving, sweet potato is richest in Vitamin A (RAE) at 122% of the Daily Value, Vitamin C (25%), Manganese (25%), Copper (20%).

Is sweet potato healthy?

By nutrient density, sweet potato scores 156 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
