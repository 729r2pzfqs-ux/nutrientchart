Soy milk nutrition facts

Soy milk (1 cup, 243 g) has 104 calories, 6.3 g protein, 0.5 g fiber, 3.6 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin B12 (86% DV), copper (45% DV), riboflavin (34% DV) and a good source of vitamin A and vitamin D.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in soy milk?

Soy milk has 104 calories per 1 cup (243 g), or 43 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in soy milk?

6.3 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 13% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is soy milk rich in?

Per serving, soy milk is richest in Vitamin B12 at 86% of the Daily Value, Copper (45%), Riboflavin (B2) (34%), Calcium (23%).

Is soy milk healthy?

By nutrient density, soy milk scores 47 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
