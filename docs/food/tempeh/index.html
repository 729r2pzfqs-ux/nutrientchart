Tempeh nutrition facts

Tempeh (1 cup, 166 g) has 319 calories, 33.7 g protein, 17.9 g fat, 12.7 g carbohydrate. It is an excellent source of copper (103% DV), manganese (94% DV), protein (67% DV) and a good source of zinc and potassium.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in tempeh?

Tempeh has 319 calories per 1 cup (166 g), or 192 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in tempeh?

33.7 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 67% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is tempeh rich in?

Per serving, tempeh is richest in Copper at 103% of the Daily Value, Manganese (94%), Protein (67%), Riboflavin (B2) (46%).

Is tempeh healthy?

By nutrient density, tempeh scores 41 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
