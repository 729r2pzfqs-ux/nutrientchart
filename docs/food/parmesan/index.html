Parmesan nutrition facts

Parmesan (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 111 calories, 10.1 g protein, 0 g fiber, 7.1 g fat. It is an excellent source of calcium (26% DV), protein (20% DV) and a good source of phosphorus and vitamin B12.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in parmesan?

Parmesan has 111 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 392 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in parmesan?

10.1 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 20% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is parmesan rich in?

Per serving, parmesan is richest in Calcium at 26% of the Daily Value, Protein (20%), Phosphorus (16%), Vitamin B12 (14%).

Is parmesan healthy?

By nutrient density, parmesan scores 20 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is pro-inflammatory profile compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
