Oats nutrition facts

Oats (0.5 cup, 40.5 g) has 153 calories, 5.3 g protein, 4.1 g fiber, 2.6 g fat. It is an excellent source of manganese (64% DV), selenium (21% DV) and a good source of copper and thiamin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in oats?

Oats has 153 calories per 0.5 cup (40.5 g), or 379 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in oats?

5.3 g of protein per 0.5 cup, which is 11% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is oats rich in?

Per serving, oats is richest in Manganese at 64% of the Daily Value, Selenium (21%), Copper (18%), Thiamin (B1) (16%).

Is oats healthy?

By nutrient density, oats scores 33 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
