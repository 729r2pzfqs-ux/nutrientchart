Almonds nutrition facts

Almonds (1 oz (23 whole kernels), 28.35 g) has 164 calories, 6.1 g protein, 3.1 g fiber, 14.5 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin E (48% DV), copper (29% DV), manganese (26% DV) and a good source of magnesium and protein.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in almonds?

Almonds has 164 calories per 1 oz (23 whole kernels) (28.35 g), or 579 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in almonds?

6.1 g of protein per 1 oz (23 whole kernels), which is 12% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is almonds rich in?

Per serving, almonds is richest in Vitamin E at 48% of the Daily Value, Copper (29%), Manganese (26%), Riboflavin (B2) (25%).

Is almonds healthy?

By nutrient density, almonds scores 59 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
