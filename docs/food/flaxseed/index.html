Flaxseed nutrition facts

Flaxseed (1 tbsp, whole, 10.3 g) has 55 calories, 1.9 g protein, 2.4 g fiber, 3.8 g fat. It is an excellent source of selenium (25% DV) and a good source of copper and manganese.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in flaxseed?

Flaxseed has 55 calories per 1 tbsp, whole (10.3 g), or 534 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in flaxseed?

1.9 g of protein per 1 tbsp, whole, which is 3.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is flaxseed rich in?

Per serving, flaxseed is richest in Selenium at 25% of the Daily Value, Copper (15%), Manganese (11%), Magnesium (9.1%).

Is flaxseed healthy?

By nutrient density, flaxseed scores 48 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
