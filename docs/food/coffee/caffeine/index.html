About caffeine

Caffeine is a stimulant found in coffee, tea, cocoa and energy drinks. There is no Daily Value; the FDA cites 400 mg a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects in healthy adults. Values are milligrams per serving.

Foods with more caffeine than coffee

  1. Beverages, coffee (brewed, prepared with tap water): 95 mg per 1 cup (8 fl oz)
  2. Beverages, Energy Drink with carbonated water and high fructose corn syrup: 91 mg per 8 fl oz
  3. Beverages, Energy Drink (Monster, fortified with vitamins C, B2, B3, B6, B12): 79 mg per 8 fl oz
  4. Beverages, Energy drink (FULL THROTTLE): 79 mg per 8 fl oz
  5. Beverages, Energy drink (ROCKSTAR): 79 mg per 8 fl oz
