Kale nutrition facts

Kale (1 cup, 21 g) has 7 calories, 0.6 g protein, 0.9 g fiber, 0.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (68% DV), vitamin C (22% DV).

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in kale?

Kale has 7 calories per 1 cup (21 g), or 35 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in kale?

0.6 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 1.2% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is kale rich in?

Per serving, kale is richest in Vitamin K at 68% of the Daily Value, Vitamin C (22%), Manganese (8.4%), Vitamin A (RAE) (5.6%).

Is kale healthy?

By nutrient density, kale scores 357 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
