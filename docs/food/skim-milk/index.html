Skim milk nutrition facts

Skim milk (1 cup, 245 g) has 83 calories, 8.4 g protein, 0 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin B12 (59% DV), calcium (25% DV), riboflavin (25% DV) and a good source of pantothenic acid and vitamin A.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in skim milk?

Skim milk has 83 calories per 1 cup (245 g), or 34 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in skim milk?

8.4 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 17% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is skim milk rich in?

Per serving, skim milk is richest in Vitamin B12 at 59% of the Daily Value, Calcium (25%), Riboflavin (B2) (25%), Phosphorus (21%).

Is skim milk healthy?

By nutrient density, skim milk scores 55 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
