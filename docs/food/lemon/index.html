Lemon nutrition facts

Lemon (1 fruit (2-1/8" dia), 58 g) has 17 calories, 0.6 g protein, 1.6 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (34% DV).

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in lemon?

Lemon has 17 calories per 1 fruit (2-1/8" dia) (58 g), or 29 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in lemon?

0.6 g of protein per 1 fruit (2-1/8" dia), which is 1.3% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is lemon rich in?

Per serving, lemon is richest in Vitamin C at 34% of the Daily Value, Fiber (5.8%), Vitamin B6 (2.7%), Copper (2.4%).

Is lemon healthy?

By nutrient density, lemon scores 163 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
