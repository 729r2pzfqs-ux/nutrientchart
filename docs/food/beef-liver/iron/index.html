Iron in beef liver

Beef liver has 5.6 mg of iron per 3 oz (85 g), which is 31% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 6.5 mg. Among meat and poultry it ranks #1 of 50 for iron per serving, and #8 of 100 across all whole foods.

About iron

Iron carries oxygen in haemoglobin. The Daily Value is 18 mg. Heme iron from meat, fish and poultry is absorbed far better than non-heme iron from plants; vitamin C eaten at the same meal improves plant-iron absorption.

Foods with more iron than beef liver

  1. Soybeans, mature cooked (boiled, without salt): 8.8 mg per 1 cup (49% DV)
  2. Beans, white (mature seeds, canned): 7.8 mg per 1 cup (44% DV)
  3. Mollusks, oyster (eastern, wild, cooked, moist heat): 7.8 mg per 3 oz (44% DV)
  4. Tofu, raw (regular, prepared with calcium sulfate): 6.6 mg per 0.5 cup (37% DV)
  5. Lentils, mature seeds (cooked, boiled, without salt): 6.6 mg per 1 cup (37% DV)

Frequently asked questions

How much iron is in beef liver?

Beef liver has 5.6 mg of iron per 3 oz (85 g), which is 31% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 6.5 mg.

Is beef liver high in iron?

Yes. At 31% of the Daily Value per serving, beef liver counts as an excellent source under FDA labeling rules (10% is 'good', 20% is 'high'). Among meat and poultry it ranks #1 of 50 for iron per serving, and #8 of 100 across all whole foods.

How much iron do I need a day?

The FDA Daily Value is 18 mg. One 3 oz of beef liver covers 31% of it.
