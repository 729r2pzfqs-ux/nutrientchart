Broccoli nutrition facts

Broccoli (1 cup chopped, 91 g) has 31 calories, 2.3 g protein, 2.2 g fiber, 0.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (92% DV), vitamin K (77% DV) and a good source of folate and pantothenic acid.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in broccoli?

Broccoli has 31 calories per 1 cup chopped (91 g), or 34 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in broccoli?

2.3 g of protein per 1 cup chopped, which is 4.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is broccoli rich in?

Per serving, broccoli is richest in Vitamin C at 92% of the Daily Value, Vitamin K (77%), Folate (DFE) (15%), Pantothenic acid (B5) (11%).

Is broccoli healthy?

By nutrient density, broccoli scores 186 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
