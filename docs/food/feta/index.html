Feta nutrition facts

Feta (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 75 calories, 5.6 g protein, 0 g fiber, 5.4 g fat. It is a good source of vitamin B12 and protein.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in feta?

Feta has 75 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 265 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in feta?

5.6 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 11% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is feta rich in?

Per serving, feta is richest in Vitamin B12 at 20% of the Daily Value, Protein (11%), Riboflavin (B2) (9.7%), Calcium (8.1%).

Is feta healthy?

By nutrient density, feta scores -4 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is pro-inflammatory profile compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
