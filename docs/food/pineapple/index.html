Pineapple nutrition facts

Pineapple (1 cup, chunks, 165 g) has 82 calories, 0.8 g protein, 1.5 g fiber, 0.3 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin C (107% DV), manganese (62% DV) and a good source of copper and vitamin B6.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in pineapple?

Pineapple has 82 calories per 1 cup, chunks (165 g), or 50 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in pineapple?

0.8 g of protein per 1 cup, chunks, which is 1.5% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is pineapple rich in?

Per serving, pineapple is richest in Vitamin C at 107% of the Daily Value, Manganese (62%), Copper (17%), Vitamin B6 (11%).

Is pineapple healthy?

By nutrient density, pineapple scores 85 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
