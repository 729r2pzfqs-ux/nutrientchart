Spinach nutrition facts

Spinach (1 cup, 30 g) has 7 calories, 0.9 g protein, 0.5 g fiber, 0.2 g fat. It is an excellent source of vitamin K (121% DV) and a good source of vitamin A and vitamin C.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in spinach?

Spinach has 7 calories per 1 cup (30 g), or 23 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in spinach?

0.9 g of protein per 1 cup, which is 1.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is spinach rich in?

Per serving, spinach is richest in Vitamin K at 121% of the Daily Value, Vitamin A (RAE) (10%), Vitamin C (10%), Folate (DFE) (8.5%).

Is spinach healthy?

By nutrient density, spinach scores 470 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
