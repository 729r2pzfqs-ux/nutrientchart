Potassium in spinach

Spinach has 138 mg of potassium per 1 cup (30 g), which is 2.9% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 460 mg.

About potassium

Potassium is an electrolyte that helps regulate fluid balance, nerve signals and muscle contractions, and a higher intake is linked to lower blood pressure. The Daily Value is 4,700 mg, which most people do not reach. Beans, potatoes, leafy greens, fish, dairy and fruit are the main sources.

Foods with more potassium than spinach

  1. Beet greens, cooked (boiled, drained, without salt): 1,309 mg per 1 cup (1" pieces) (28% DV)
  2. Beans, adzuki (mature seeds, cooked, boiled, without salt): 1,224 mg per 1 cup (26% DV)
  3. Beans, white (mature seeds, canned): 1,189 mg per 1 cup (25% DV)
  4. Avocados, raw (Florida): 1,067 mg per 1 fruit without skin and seeds (23% DV)
  5. Soybeans, green (cooked, boiled, drained, without salt): 970 mg per 1 cup (21% DV)

Frequently asked questions

How much potassium is in spinach?

Spinach has 138 mg of potassium per 1 cup (30 g), which is 2.9% of the Daily Value. Per 100 g it contains 460 mg.

Is spinach high in potassium?

Not especially. One serving provides 2.9% of the Daily Value, below the 10% FDA threshold for a 'good source'.

How much potassium do I need a day?

The FDA Daily Value is 4,700 mg. One 1 cup of spinach covers 2.9% of it.
