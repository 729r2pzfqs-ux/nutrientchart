Sesame seeds nutrition facts

Sesame seeds (1 tbsp, 9 g) has 52 calories, 1.6 g protein, 1.1 g fiber, 4.5 g fat. It is an excellent source of copper (41% DV).

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in sesame seeds?

Sesame seeds has 52 calories per 1 tbsp (9 g), or 573 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in sesame seeds?

1.6 g of protein per 1 tbsp, which is 3.2% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is sesame seeds rich in?

Per serving, sesame seeds is richest in Copper at 41% of the Daily Value, Manganese (9.6%), Magnesium (7.5%), Iron (7.3%).

Is sesame seeds healthy?

By nutrient density, sesame seeds scores 51 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
