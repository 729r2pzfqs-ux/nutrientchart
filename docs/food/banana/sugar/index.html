Sugars in banana

Banana has 14.4 g of sugars per 1 medium (7" to 7-7/8" long) (118 g). Per 100 g it contains 12.2 g.

About sugars

Total sugars include both the sugars naturally present in fruit, milk and vegetables and any added sugars. USDA SR Legacy does not separate added sugars, so this list ranks by total sugars; whole foods with the least are listed first.
