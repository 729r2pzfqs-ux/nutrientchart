Banana nutrition facts

Banana (1 medium (7" to 7-7/8" long), 118 g) has 105 calories, 0.9 g protein, 2 g fiber, 0.3 g fat. It is a good source of vitamin C and vitamin B6.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in banana?

Banana has 105 calories per 1 medium (7" to 7-7/8" long) (118 g), or 89 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in banana?

0.9 g of protein per 1 medium (7" to 7-7/8" long), which is 1.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is banana rich in?

Per serving, banana is richest in Vitamin C at 16% of the Daily Value, Vitamin B6 (15%), Copper (13%), Manganese (13%).

Is banana healthy?

By nutrient density, banana scores 14 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
