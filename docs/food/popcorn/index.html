Popcorn nutrition facts

Popcorn (1 oz, 28.35 g) has 110 calories, 3.7 g protein, 4.1 g fiber, 1.3 g fat. It is a good source of fiber and manganese.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in popcorn?

Popcorn has 110 calories per 1 oz (28.35 g), or 387 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in popcorn?

3.7 g of protein per 1 oz, which is 7.3% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is popcorn rich in?

Per serving, popcorn is richest in Fiber at 15% of the Daily Value, Manganese (14%), Magnesium (9.7%), Copper (8.3%).

Is popcorn healthy?

By nutrient density, popcorn scores 35 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
