Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in dates?

Dates has 66 calories per 1 date, pitted (24 g), or 277 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in dates?

0.4 g of protein per 1 date, pitted, which is 0.9% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is dates rich in?

Per serving, dates is richest in Copper at 9.7% of the Daily Value, Fiber (5.7%), Pantothenic acid (B5) (3.9%), Potassium (3.6%).

Is dates healthy?

By nutrient density, dates scores -24 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
