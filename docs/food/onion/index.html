Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in onion?

Onion has 44 calories per 1 medium (2-1/2" dia) (110 g), or 40 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in onion?

1.2 g of protein per 1 medium (2-1/2" dia), which is 2.4% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is onion rich in?

Per serving, onion is richest in Vitamin C at 9% of the Daily Value, Vitamin B6 (7.8%), Fiber (6.7%), Manganese (6.2%).

Is onion healthy?

By nutrient density, onion scores 40 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
