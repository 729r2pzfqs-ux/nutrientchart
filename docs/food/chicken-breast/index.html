Chicken breast nutrition facts

Chicken breast (3 oz, 85 g) has 140 calories, 26.4 g protein, 0 g fiber, 3 g fat. It is an excellent source of niacin (73% DV), protein (53% DV), selenium (43% DV) and a good source of pantothenic acid and phosphorus.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in chicken breast?

Chicken breast has 140 calories per 3 oz (85 g), or 165 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in chicken breast?

26.4 g of protein per 3 oz, which is 53% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is chicken breast rich in?

Per serving, chicken breast is richest in Niacin (B3) at 73% of the Daily Value, Protein (53%), Selenium (43%), Vitamin B6 (30%).

Is chicken breast healthy?

By nutrient density, chicken breast scores 46 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is below average compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
