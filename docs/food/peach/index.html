Peach nutrition facts

Peach (1 medium (2-2/3" dia), 150 g) has 58 calories, 1.4 g protein, 2.2 g fiber, 0.4 g fat. It is a good source of copper.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in peach?

Peach has 58 calories per 1 medium (2-2/3" dia) (150 g), or 39 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in peach?

1.4 g of protein per 1 medium (2-2/3" dia), which is 2.7% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is peach rich in?

Per serving, peach is richest in Copper at 13% of the Daily Value, Fiber (8%), Niacin (B3) (7.6%), Vitamin E (7.3%).

Is peach healthy?

By nutrient density, peach scores 22 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is strongly anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
