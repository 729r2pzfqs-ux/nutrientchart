White bread nutrition facts

White bread (1 slice, 29 g) has 77 calories, 2.6 g protein, 0.8 g fiber, 1 g fat. It is a good source of selenium and thiamin.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories are in white bread?

White bread has 77 calories per 1 slice (29 g), or 266 calories per 100 g, according to USDA FoodData Central.

How much protein is in white bread?

2.6 g of protein per 1 slice, which is 5.1% of the 50 g Daily Value.

What is white bread rich in?

Per serving, white bread is richest in Selenium at 12% of the Daily Value, Thiamin (B1) (12%), Folate (DFE) (11%), Niacin (B3) (11%).

Is white bread healthy?

By nutrient density, white bread scores 18 on the NRF9.3 index (nine nutrients to encourage minus three to limit, per 100 kcal). Its nutrient profile is anti-inflammatory compared with other whole foods. See the methodology page for how both scores work; this is data, not medical advice.
